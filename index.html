Meet The ONE · the first car built for new drivers

Your first car should also be your best driving teacher.

SAFEDRIVE ONE is a compact electric car with a built-in AI coach. It talks you through every trip, watches the road with you and helps you build safe habits from day one.

Explore the carWatch the video

Why new drivers choose The ONE

The first year behind the wheel is when drivers learn the most and when mistakes are most likely. We designed every part of The ONE around that year.

A coach in every trip

A calm AI voice gives short, helpful tips, such as “school zone ahead, slow down”, and praises good driving.

Safety that steps in

Automatic braking, lane help and a 360° camera watch the road even when you miss something.

Clean electric power

Zero exhaust emissions, low running costs and quiet driving for UAE cities.

Peace of mind for families

The Parent App shares a weekly safety score, not your live location, so trust grows on both sides.
