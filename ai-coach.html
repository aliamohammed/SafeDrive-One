Feature 2 · AI Driving Coach

A calm voice that rides with you on every trip.

The AI coach watches the road with you and gives short, friendly tips at the right moment. It learns how you drive and helps you improve, one trip at a time.

How the coach works

  1. It seesCameras and sensors read the road, signs, speed and the distance to other cars.
  2. It understandsThe AI compares what is happening with safe driving patterns and your own habits.
  3. It speaksIt gives one short tip at a time, only when it helps, and never during a hard manoeuvre.
  4. It reviewsAfter each trip you get a short summary with one thing done well and one thing to practise.

What the coach might say

Approaching a school zone“School zone ahead. Let’s slow down and watch for children.”
Following too closely“The car in front is close. Leave a little more space.”
After a smooth trip“Great trip! Your braking was smooth all the way home.”
Late at night“You have been driving for two hours. A short break would help you stay alert.”

Three coaching levels

As you improve, the coach talks less and trusts you more.

Learner

Frequent, simple tips and lower speed settings. Best for the first months.

Improving

Tips only for new or tricky situations, plus weekly goals.

Confident

The coach stays quiet unless something important happens.

Good to know: the coach helps, but you are always the driver. Stay alert and follow the road rules.
