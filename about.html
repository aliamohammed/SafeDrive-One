About SAFEDRIVE ONE

We started with one question: why is the first car not made for the first year?

SAFEDRIVE ONE is a UAE car company with one goal: to help every new driver become a safe, confident driver. We build one car, The ONE, and we build it only for learners and new drivers.

Our story

Most cars are designed for experienced drivers. New drivers get the same car, the same speed and the same distractions, but without the years of practice. Our founders, a team of engineers, driving instructors and designers, saw this gap and asked a simple question: what if the car could teach?

In 2026 we began designing The ONE in Dubai. Every feature started with a real moment new drivers find hard: joining a busy highway, driving at night, a sudden sandstorm or the temptation to check a phone.

Today The ONE combines an AI coach, smart safety systems and clean electric power in one friendly, affordable car.

Mission and values

Our mission: to help every new driver become a safe, confident driver, and to reduce accidents caused by inexperience.

Safety first

Every design choice is tested against one question: does it help a new driver stay safe?

Teach, do not punish

Our AI coach encourages and explains. It never shames the driver for a mistake.

Respect privacy

Drivers own their data. Parents see safety scores, not live location.

Care for the planet

The ONE is fully electric and uses recycled materials inside the cabin.
