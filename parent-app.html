Feature 5 · Parent App

Support your new driver without watching every move.

The free SAFEDRIVE ONE app lets families set learner settings and see a weekly safety score. It helps parents support young drivers while respecting their privacy.

What the app does

Weekly safety score

A simple score out of 100 based on braking, speed, phone use and night trips.

Learner settings

Set a top speed, switch on phone lock and choose the coaching level.

Shared goals

Parents and drivers agree on one goal each week, like smoother braking.

Privacy by design

No live location tracking. The driver can see everything the parent sees.

Settings that grow with the driver

In the first months, families may choose stricter settings. As the safety score improves, the app suggests relaxing them, so the young driver earns more freedom.

Both the parent and the driver get the same weekly report. This keeps the app about learning together, not about spying.

Our privacy promise: trip details stay with the driver. Parents see scores and settings only.
