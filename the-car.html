Feature 1 · The Car

Small outside. Smart inside. Made for your first year.

The ONE is a compact electric hatchback. It is easy to park, easy to see out of and full of technology that helps you learn.

Designed to feel easy

Big windows and a high seat give you a clear view of the road. Short front and back ends make parking simple, and the 360° camera shows everything around you on the main screen.

Inside, there are no confusing menus. One large touchscreen shows the road, your coach and your trip. Physical buttons control the most important things, like hazard lights and air conditioning, so your eyes stay on the road.

The cabin uses seat fabric made from recycled bottles and a cool-touch dashboard designed for the UAE summer.

How The ONE works

Forward sensors watch the road, an AI computer runs the driving coach, a flat battery sits low in the floor for stability, and an electric motor drives the wheels.
